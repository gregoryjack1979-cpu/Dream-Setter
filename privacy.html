Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Dream Setter Inc. (“Dream Setter,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides AI-assisted appointment-setting services. This policy explains what information we collect through dreamsetter.net (the “Site”) and in the course of providing our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a demo, book a call, or contact us, we collect what you enter: your name, company, phone number, email address, approximate monthly lead volume, and any notes you include.

Calculator inputs. The scenario calculator on the Site runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored by us unless you choose to copy or download them and share them with us yourself.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps) to operate and secure the Site. The Site itself does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Communications. If you call, text, or email us, we keep a record of that communication, including call recordings or transcripts where permitted by law and where you have been notified.

How we use information

  • To respond to your demo request and schedule and conduct calls with you.
  • To provide, configure, and support appointment-setting services you have engaged us for.
  • To send service-related messages, such as appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.
  • To improve the Site and our services, and to keep them secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Text messages (SMS) and calls

If you provide your mobile number and agree to receive text messages, Dream Setter Inc. may send you SMS messages related to your demo request, appointment scheduling, reminders, and customer care.

  • Consent. You consent by checking the consent box on our demo request form, by booking an appointment through our scheduling page, or by texting us first.
  • Message frequency varies depending on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.
  • No sharing of mobile opt-in data. Your phone number and text-message consent will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any service. If you provide a phone number, we may also call you about your request. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes where permitted by law.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as our customer relationship management and communications platform, email and telephone providers, scheduling tools, and website hosting. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Dream Setter, our clients, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Information we process for our clients

When a business engages Dream Setter to contact its leads, that business controls the lead data and is responsible for having the appropriate consent to contact those individuals. We process that data on the client’s behalf and under the client’s instructions, as set out in our service agreement. If you have received a call or message from us on behalf of one of our clients and have questions about your data, contact us and we will direct your request appropriately.

Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to fulfil the purposes described above, to maintain business records, and to meet legal obligations. Demo requests that do not lead to a business relationship are typically retained for no longer than 24 months.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

  • You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any such email.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

The Site and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Contact us

For questions about this policy or your information, contact Dream Setter Inc. at the mailing address above, or use the contact details on our Book a demo page.
