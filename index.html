See the math behind the model +

Appointments = leads × booking rate. Net sales = appointments × booked-to-net-sale rate. Marketing cost per sale = monthly marketing spend ÷ net sales.

The profit allowance assumes your baseline margin already deducts your original marketing cost per sale. With marketing spend held fixed, the model adds the resulting marketing efficiency once, plus the baseline profit allowance on added revenue. It then adds only the staffing costs you remove and subtracts the AI fee on all modeled appointments and any extra costs.

At the default values: $7,500 marketing improvement + $3,750 additional profit allowance + $6,450 staffing removed − $3,250 AI fees = $14,450 per month. This is equivalent to $37,500 additional revenue × 30% before-marketing allowance + $3,200 net staffing savings. These are two ways to calculate the same benefit; do not add them together.

If you keep all current staff, the default example becomes $8,000 per month. Costs, conversion rates, service scope, and billing terms must be confirmed for your business.

A few straight answers

Know what
you’re comparing.

What do you need from me to get started? +

Your lead source, your qualifying questions and script, the calendar or CRM where appointments should land, and the hours and days you want calls made. Setup is a working session with your team, followed by test calls before anything goes live.

Does this replace my whole team? +

That is your call. Many businesses keep their closers and let Dream Setter handle first contact, double dials, voicemail, and follow-up. The calculator lets you model either choice: enter the staffing cost you would actually remove, or $0 to keep everyone.

Which businesses is this for? +

Any business that buys or generates leads and needs them called and booked consistently. The sample numbers model a high-ticket sale with a $10,000 average and a 25% booked-to-net-sale rate; replace them with your own.

Are these guaranteed savings? +

No. This is an editable model. The sample 13% booking rate is a hypothetical target, not a measured Dream Setter result. Your actual outcome depends on your inputs and execution.

What if I keep my staff? +

Put $0 in “Monthly staffing cost you could remove.” The model only counts costs that actually go away. Add any oversight or other new costs, too.

Is $50 the final service price? +

It is the illustrative per-booked-appointment fee used in the example. The model charges it on all scenario appointments. Final pricing, billable-appointment definitions, setup and usage charges are confirmed on your demo call.

Will AI follow every step perfectly? +

AI needs configuration, testing, and monitoring. Set your script, double-dial behavior, voicemail rules, and schedule, then check the call activity. A clear process and ongoing oversight matter.
